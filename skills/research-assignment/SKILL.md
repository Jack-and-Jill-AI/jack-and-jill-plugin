# Research an assignment

Use this skill before clarifying a new hiring need or searching for candidates.

Research should answer obvious questions before they consume the principal's time and sharpen the questions that remain.

## Gather evidence

Look at:

- the company's product, stage, website, launches, funding, and recent news;
- the role source, including the job description and material the organization supplied;
- internal documents the user has made available;
- relevant current team members and what their experience implies about the bar and the gap.

Do not treat a job description, company claim, or profile inference as independently verified. Keep source links or source names beside material claims.

## Write the research context

Create one self-contained Markdown document with:

- what the company does and where it appears to be going;
- what the role must achieve;
- what internal material adds;
- what the current team suggests about the bar;
- separate lists for sourced facts and assumptions;
- the questions that remain for the principal.

Save the document with `revise_context`, using `kind="research"`. Show or summarize it to the principal so they can correct it while corrections are cheap.

Fold corrections into `research`, then update `shared`, `private`, or `working` when they change the role, confidential evaluation bar, or next action.

Research is complete when each load-bearing claim has a source or is clearly marked as an assumption, and the remaining questions are specific enough to change the search plan.
